Highlights
(Elsevier: 3-5 bullet points, each <= 85 characters including spaces)

- First thermoelectric transport study of hexagonal CrN nanoribbons (no DFT).
- Half-metallic edges yield a 100% spin-polarized thermoelectric current.
- ZT peaks at a sharp band edge: ZT ~ 0.2-0.3 at 300 K, rising with temperature.
- Design rules from edge type, ribbon width, doping window and temperature.
- Self-consistent edge magnetism reproduces the DMRG magnetic CrN edges.
