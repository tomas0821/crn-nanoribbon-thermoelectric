Highlights
(Elsevier: 3-5 bullet points, each <= 85 characters including spaces)

- First thermoelectric (Landauer) study of CrN nanoribbons, no ab-initio input.
- Minority channel gapped over ~4 eV: transport is 100% spin-polarized when gated.
- ZT = 0.06-0.15 at 300 K, every optimum inside the fully spin-polarized window.
- A minimal orbital basis fabricates a sharp edge and overestimates ZT fivefold.
- Antiparallel magnetizations switch the thermocurrent off: a thermal spin valve.
